Mobile Game Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy for Blind Chicken

App Name: Blind Chicken Package: com.blind_bird Developer: warqa603 Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Blind Chicken ("we," "our," or "the Game"), developed by warqa603. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our mobile game application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you download, install, and play Blind Chicken, how that information is utilized, and your choices regarding your personal privacy under applicable international laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and Google Play Developer Policies.

Quick Summary: Blind Chicken does not require any account registration. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or personal identity. All game progress is stored strictly on your device. Third-party advertising (Google AdMob) is integrated to support free gameplay.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Blind Chicken is built as an offline-first casual arcade runner. We do not require or collect:

  • Personal Identity Information: No names, user handles, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Account Credentials: No sign-up, passwords, or social media logins (Facebook, Google Sign-In, etc.).
  • Sensitive Data: No biometric data, financial accounts, credit cards, or government IDs.
  • Private User Files: No access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or documents.
  • Precise GPS Location: We do not request or track precise geolocation coordinates.

3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

To provide a seamless gaming experience, the Game saves your gameplay state locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences storage:

  • Level & World Progress: Highest unlocked level (out of 105 stages) and unlocked worlds (out of 7 worlds).
  • In-Game Currencies: Collected white eggs and converted golden eggs.
  • Lives System: Current heart count and replenishment timestamps.
  • Player Preferences: Audio toggle states (sound effects, background music) and haptic vibration preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device. It is never transmitted, synced, or sold to any remote database. You can clear this progress at any time via the in-game Reset button or by clearing the app data in your Android device settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Blind Chicken free to play for everyone, we use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous technical information from your device in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Coarse Location: IP address used strictly to deliver region-appropriate advertisements and prevent fraudulent traffic.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and rewarded video completion events.

For detailed information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time directly in your Android device: Settings ➜ Google ➜ Ads ➜ Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. In-Game Economy & Rewarded Videos

Blind Chicken features an optional rewarded video ad system:

  • Voluntary Viewing: Rewarded video ads are strictly optional and triggered solely when you explicitly tap to watch an ad (such as earning a second chance revive or replenishing hearts).
  • No Real-Money Purchases: The game does not feature real-money loot boxes, pay-to-win gambling mechanics, or mandatory paid subscriptions.

6. Device Permissions

The Game requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary for operation on Android:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load advertisements from Google AdMob and verify ad delivery.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to check whether your device has an active internet connection.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during jumps, crashes, and button presses (can be disabled at any time via the in-game settings toggle).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Blind Chicken is designed for a general, family-friendly audience of all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • AdMob ad requests within the app are configured with appropriate child-directed and family-safe tags where required by Google Play policy.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that any personal information has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately at bestmodels603@gmail.com, and we will take prompt action.

8. International User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

For Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK (GDPR):

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we do not store personal data on any server, your in-game data can be deleted instantly by uninstalling the application or clearing the application cache. For advertising data handled by Google, you may exercise your rights through Google's privacy portal.

For California Residents (CCPA / CPRA):

We do not "sell" your personal information. We do not exchange user data for monetary consideration. You have the right to request deletion of any collected data and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Data Security

Because all game data resides on your physical device, your progress is protected by Android's application sandbox security model. Communication with Google AdMob servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS).
