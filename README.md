## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Blind Chicken**, developed by **warqa603**. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details what information is collected, how it is handled, and your rights when downloading and playing Blind Chicken on Android devices.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
Blind Chicken is built as an offline-first casual arcade game. We do NOT collect:
- Personal names, emails, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
- User accounts, passwords, or social network credentials.
- Sensitive personal data, banking details, or financial accounts.
- Access to private photos, camera, microphone, or user documents.
- Precise GPS coordinates or location tracking.

## 3. Local Data Storage on Device
Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences`:
- Highest unlocked level (out of 105 levels) and worlds (out of 7 worlds).
- Total collected white eggs and golden eggs.
- Lives / hearts count and automatic replenishment timers.
- Audio and haptic vibration preferences.

This data is never transmitted to any private server and remains entirely on your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game uses **Google AdMob** to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to keep the game free to play.

AdMob may automatically process pseudonymous technical device identifiers:
- Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
- Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
- Coarse location (IP address) for regional ad serving and fraud prevention.
- Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, rewarded completions).

Review Google's Privacy Policy:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

**Opt-out of Personalized Ads:**
You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings: `Settings ➜ Google ➜ Ads ➜ Reset advertising ID` or `Delete advertising ID`.

## 5. In-Game Economy & Rewarded Ads
- Watching rewarded video ads is strictly voluntary (e.g., to revive or earn extra golden eggs).
- The game contains no real-money gambling, paid loot boxes, or mandatory subscriptions.

## 6. Device Permissions
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Loads advertisements from Google AdMob.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Checks internet connectivity for ad delivery.
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Provides optional tactile haptic feedback during jumps and collisions.

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)
- Blind Chicken is rated for all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).
- We comply with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- Ad requests are configured with family-safe parameters.

## 8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
- **GDPR (EEA/UK):** You can delete your local data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage. Ad preferences can be managed via Google's settings.
- **CCPA (California):** We do not sell user personal data.
